# Company Manage

## Example - mobile app development


The company has a mobile app team responsible for developing mobile apps.
The mobile app team is equipped with three managers.
Every time a development task is received, the three managers first hold a meeting together to discuss the requirements. Then, they meet with the employees to explain the scope and details of the task, and the employees vote to select the manager for this specific task. The manager with the most votes will manage the development task, and all employees will work with them. If there is a tie in votes, the system will determine the manager.
If there is a concurrent second task, the exact same process is repeated. A portion of the employees is assigned to work on the second task, and this group still votes to decide who manages it.
This means a single manager might lead employees across two development tasks simultaneously, or two managers might each lead a group of employees on separate development tasks.
