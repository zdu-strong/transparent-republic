# Company Manage

## Example - mobile app development


The company has a mobile app team responsible for developing mobile apps.<br/>

The mobile app team is equipped with three managers.<br/>

Every time a development task is received, the three managers first hold a meeting together to discuss the requirements.<br/>

Then, they meet with the employees to explain the scope and details of the task, and the employees vote to select the manager for this specific task.<br/>

The manager with the most votes will manage the development task, and all employees will work with them. If there is a tie in votes, the system will determine the manager.<br/>

If there is a concurrent second task, the exact same process is repeated. All employees vote to select the manager responsible for this specific task, and that manager will then select a portion of the employees to work on the task.<br/>

## Example - Salary Increase

​Before discussing each task with the employees, the three managers score the task individually. The sum of the scores evaluated by the three managers will serve as the final score for the task.<br/>

Managers who lead their team to complete the task receive 100% of the task score.<br/>

After each task is completed, the manager who led the task rates the participating team members as "Positive," "Negative," or "No Rating."<br/>

* Positive: 100% task score.

* No Rating: 50% task score.

* Negative: 25% task score.

When the company decides to award pay raises, it may award either employees or managers with higher performance scores.<br/>

## Example - Promotion

When a role opens up, the company may fill it via external hiring or internal promotion of top performers. For internal promotions, candidates may be selected from among employees or managers with higher performance scores.<br/>
