#  Healthcare Coverage

Medical insurance is divided into three categories: minor illnesses, moderate illnesses, and severe illnesses.<br/>

For minor illnesses, the country government covers 70% of the medical expenses, while individuals cover 30%. Citizens, immigrants, and residents are all eligible for this treatment.<br/>

For moderate illnesses, the country government covers the entire cost. Only citizens and immigrants are eligible for this treatment, residents are also eligible after receiving basic living allowance for 3,552 days.<br/>

For severe illnesses, the country government sets a specific reimbursement limit for medical expenses. Expenses within the limit are covered by the country government. If the treatment exceeds the limit, the excess portion is borne by the individual. Only citizens and immigrants are eligible for this treatment, residents are also eligible after receiving basic living allowance for 3,552 days.<br/>
