#  Healthcare Coverage

Medical coverage is divided into three categories: minor illnesses, moderate illnesses, and severe illnesses.<br/>

For minor illnesses, individuals pay out-of-pocket for any amount under $100 per week, and the country government covers the excess.<br/>

For moderate illnesses, the country government covers the entire cost.<br/>

For severe illnesses, the country government sets a specific reimbursement limit for medical expenses. Expenses within the limit are covered by the country government. If the treatment exceeds the limit, the excess portion is borne by the individual.<br/>

Citizens and immigrants are eligible for full medical coverage.<br/>

Residents are entitled to medical coverage for minor illnesses and will receive full medical coverage after receiving 3,552 days of basic living allowance. Prior to that, residents can pay out of pocket or purchase commercial medical insurance to treat moderate and severe illnesses.<br/>
