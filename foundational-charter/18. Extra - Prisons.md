# Prisons

## Prisons are divided into high-security areas and low-security areas.<br/>

### Low-security areas

Inmates in low-security areas have unlimited unrestricted internet access.<br/>

Prisoners in low-security areas may choose each day to use prisoner-only facilities such as libraries, basketball courts, football fields, and other activity spaces.<br/>

Low-security prisoners are required to return to their cells only during designated sleeping hours.<br/>

### High-security areas

Inmates in high-security areas have thirty minutes of unrestricted internet access each day. If their relatives are unable to contact them, they may sue the government in court.<br/>

High-security prisoners get only 1 hour of yard time each day and must stay in their cells for the rest of the time.<br/>

Prisoners in high-security areas may be transferred to low-security areas if they commit no violations for 1 month.<br/>

## Special criminal provisions for offenses committed by prisoners inside prisons

The law shall establish special criminal provisions for offenses committed by prisoners inside prisons.<br/>

​If an inmate threatens others, the case will be heard by the court. The inmate will be sentenced to an additional one month of prison time, transferred to high-security areas, and assigned a dedicated correctional officer for supervision. The inmate may interact with other inmates under the officer’s supervision, and the officer must intervene to stop the inmate from using threatening language.<br/>

If an inmate attempts to harm or has harmed others, the case will be heard by the court. The inmate will be sentenced to an additional three months of imprisonment, transferred to High-security areas, and assigned three dedicated correctional officers for 1 month of supervision, during which time the inmate will be prohibited from approaching other inmates.<br/>

## Warden - The Tri-Vector Commander

The country establishes a prison department, and the minister of prisons sends a Tri-Vector Commander of three officials to manage each new prison.<br/>

The three officials vote to elect the warden, while the other two officials serve as prison commissioners. If the vote totals differ, the official with the most votes becomes the warden. If the vote count is tied, the official ranked higher by the system will be elected as the prison warden.<br/>

The warden is responsible for hiring staff and managing the prison.<br/>

Each year, two new officials replace the two commissioners. The commissioners do not manage the prison, they only oversee it.<br/>

The three officials can vote at any time to elect a new warden.<br/>

The three officials must assess the initial warden. Each assessment shall be positive, negative, or withheld.<br/>

For example, A is the warden, and B and C are commissioners.<br/>

A, B, and C each vote for themselves, and A retains the warden position.<br/>

B votes for C. C becomes the new warden, and A becomes a commissioner.<br/>

B votes for himself again, and C remains the warden.<br/>


## Consumer Freedom and Job Opportunities

Inmates in Low-security areas can freely consume and purchase goods, having them delivered to their rooms.<br/>
The warden can partner with factories, with the warden arranging scheduled transport vehicles to ferry prisoners back and forth, and the warden making a profit from the ticket fares paid by the prisoners.<br/>

## Freedom of Transfer

Prisoners can change prisons once a month. Each prison indicates information such as its remaining capacity and which prisons it is willing to send vehicles to pick up prisoners from, and the prisoners make their choices.<br/>
