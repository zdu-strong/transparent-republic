
# Persons with Disabilities

A person with a disability—or someone acting on their behalf—submits a disability funding application through the system. Upon approval by the President and the Ministry of Finance, personnel are dispatched to help hire a caregiver and arrange for a vehicle.<br/>


After a period of time, the court initiates proceedings against the applicant, and the caregiver accompanies the person with a disability to the court for verification, thereby completing the process.<br/>

If the person with a disability relocates, they may submit a new application for a caregiver through the system at any time, and the state will dispatch personnel to hire a new caregiver and arrange for a vehicle.<br/>
