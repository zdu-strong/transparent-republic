
# Persons with Disabilities

The disability application provides assistance strictly for mobility and transportation.

## Disability Application

A person with a disability—or someone acting on their behalf—submits a disability funding application through the system. Upon approval by the President and the Ministry of Finance, personnel are dispatched to help hire a caregiver and arrange for a vehicle.<br/>

After a period of time, the court initiates proceedings against the applicant, and the caregiver accompanies the person with a disability to the court for verification, thereby completing the process.<br/>

If the person with a disability relocates, they may submit a new application for a caregiver through the system at any time, and the country goverment will dispatch personnel to hire a new caregiver and arrange for a vehicle.<br/>

## Mobility & Transportation

Society will no longer provide barrier-free facilities, thereby reducing construction costs.<br/>

The government allocates funds to hire dedicated caregivers, reducing caregiver costs by setting a fixed daily departure window.<br/>

For example, the window may be set from 09:00 to 10:00 daily, or finalized after consulting with the person with a disability.<br/>

Once the person with a disability departs, there is no time limit on their return, and the government covers the caregiver's wages.<br/>

Caregivers may freely use the vehicle during off-duty hours.<br/>

Persons with disabilities may replace their caregiver through the system at any time.<br/>

Caregivers self-report the start and end times of each trip, and in-vehicle video recording equipment will be installed to verify whether the reported end time differs from the actual end time by more than one hour.<br/>

A caregiver's compensation is divided into a base wage and a trip wage.<br/>

The base wage is paid daily for the fixed one-hour standby window, regardless of whether the person with a disability goes out.<br/>

The trip wage is calculated hourly, starting from the beginning of the standby window until the person with a disability returns home.<br/>

For example:<br/>

* Suppose the daily departure standby window is 09:00–10:00, and the base wage is $200.<br/>

* On a given day, they depart at 09:30 and return at 11:00. At an hourly rate of $20, the total billable trip duration is 2 hours (calculated from 09:00 to 11:00), resulting in a trip wage of $40.<br/>

* The caregiver's total pay for that day is $240.<br/>
