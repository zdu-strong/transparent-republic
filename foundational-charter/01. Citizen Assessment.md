# Citizen Assessment

No one is born a citizen. Even the children of citizens are regarded as residents at birth, and they must complete the required process before they can ultimately become citizens.

## Types of Legal Status

### 1. Citizen
Entitled to a basic living allowance, sovereign dividends, voting rights, and the right to run for office.<br/>
Holds nationality and a passport. Dual or multiple citizenship is not permitted.<br/>

### 2. Immigrant
Entitled to a basic living allowance.<br/>
Holds nationality and a passport. Dual or multiple citizenship is not permitted.<br/>

### 3. Resident

Entitled to a basic living allowance.<br/>
Resident can live there, buy land, and do business.

### 4. Anonymous

Anonymous can buy a receipt printer and start doing business.<br/>
An official shall assist the person in registering as a resident. If the person repeatedly refuses to register, they may be prosecuted.<br/>

## Citizenship Assessment Requirements

1. Must be at least 18 years old.<br/>
2. Must have held immigrant status for at least 6,574 days.<br/>
3. Must have received the Basic Living Security Grant for at least 6,574 days.<br/>
4. Must be able to speak the country’s language.<br/>
5. Must understand the political system:<br/>
A 20-question multiple-choice test, with a passing score of 60%.<br/>

## Quantity restriction

There is no limit on the number of residents or citizens.<br/>

Resources are limited, so the number of residents who can become immigrants each day is limited and determined by random selection.<br/>

There is no limit on the number of residents under one year old who may obtain immigrant status.<br/>

However, human conscience must not grow cold. A Special Rescue Department shall therefore be established to help people whose suffering receives exceptional media attention.<br/>

## Entry Risks

For residents with a criminal risk profile and anonymous individuals, the court may, upon entry—based on clear legal grounds including country of origin—issue a collective ruling sentencing them to serve 3 months in a low-security areas of prison. This measures serves to alleviate safety concerns among citizens while maintaining open borders.<br/>

## In-Country Verification

For immigrants, they only need to swipe their ID card at a self-service identity verification terminal in a store once a week, and the system will automatically record their domestic presence status. If there is no record for more than a month, the system will suspend the issuance of their basic livelihood allowance.<br/>

For residents, they only need to swipe their ID card at a self-service identity verification terminal in a store once a day, and the system will automatically record their domestic presence status. If there is no record for more than two days, the system will suspend the issuance of their basic livelihood subsidy.<br/>

This mechanism aims to guarantee the freedom of entry and exit for immigrants and residents.<br/>

The court will try cases where individuals who are abroad have someone else assist them in swiping their ID card at a store identity self-service terminal. For individuals who are abroad and unable to face trial by the court, the court will freeze their ability to update their in-country status record until they appear in court to face trial.<br/>

Domestic presence verification is solely used to determine whether to issue basic livelihood allowances and serves no other purpose.<br/>

## Identity Registration

Granting resident status to anonymous individuals is the Mayor's authority.<br/>

After the mayor grants resident status to an anonymous individual, it is re-verified by the court.<br/>

If the Mayor is unwilling to grant resident status to anonymous individuals, they can also go to the court to register as residents.<br/>

## Quarantined Governance Regions

The President will designate a portion of the governance regions as quarantined governance regions.<br/>

Residents and immigrants can only purchase land or rent housing within quarantined governance regions.<br/>

There are no distinctions among the various quarantined governance regions, and people can freely migrate between them.<br/>

Staying at hotels remains freedom, no identity registration is required for hotel stays, and there is no limit on the total duration of the stay. Hotel accommodation prices will be taxed as a commodity.<br/>

For ordinary governance regions, since they are not quarantined governance regions, immigrants cannot rent housing, but the Mayor can provide subsidies to hotels to accommodate immigrants working in essential service industries.<br/>

Sleeping on the street is a misdemeanor, and the court will try the case and sentence the offender to one month in a low-security prison.<br/>

Wearing specific religious or cultural attire is a misdemeanor. The types of attire covered by this law will be gradually supplemented through legislation. The court will try the case and sentence the offender to one month in a low-security prison.<br/>

## Supplementary Explanation

Citizens retain full citizenship rights even after leaving for another country. They can still vote, receive the basic living security payment, and receive sovereign dividends.<br/>

Immigrants and residents are entitled to the basic living security payment only when they are staying in the Transparent Republic.<br/>
