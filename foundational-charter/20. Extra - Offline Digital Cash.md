# Offline Digital Cash

By using Trusted Execution Environment and NFC technology, ID cards serve as the carrier for Offline Digital Cash, accompanied by a physical anti-misoperation toggle to enable convenient transactions.<br/>

Since anonymous individuals can also print ID cards, privacy is not a concern.<br/>

​Receipt printers can conduct financial transactions with ID cards.<br/>

Store identity self-service terminals can initialize receipt printers, synchronize receipt records from receipt printers to the server, print ID cards, and be used for voting.<br/>

People can use store identity self-service terminals to transfer funds from receipt printers or ID cards into online identity accounts, and then transfer the funds from the online identity accounts to Lumen via Lumen.<br/>
