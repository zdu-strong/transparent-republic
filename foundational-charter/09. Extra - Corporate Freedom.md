# Corporate Freedom

There is no minimum wage requirement, no social security requirement, and no restrictions on hiring or dismissal. Companies are free to hire employees as they wish.<br/>

The company bears no responsibility for employee death from overwork.<br/>
The company bears no responsibility for employee illness.<br/>
The company is responsible for employee physical disability caused by hazardous working environments.<br/>
The company is responsible for employee death caused by hazardous working environments.<br/>

Workers' wages are calculated on an hourly basis. Each day includes 4 hours of regular paid working time. Any working time exceeding 4 hours per day is counted as overtime, and any working time exceeding 16 hours per week is counted as overtime. Overtime pay is 3 times the regular wage.<br/>
