# Auction

## Land Auction

The government will list all land that can enter the market on a land auction website, with an initial price of 0.<br/>

Starting from the first bid, the auction will end after 3 days, and the highest bidder will obtain the land.<br/>

If a bidder wins the auction but fails to pay for the purchase, they will be sued in court.<br/>

Anyone may place a bid and may cancel their bid at any time.<br/>

If multiple bidders place the same bid, the auction system will rank them by randomly assigned bidding numbers to determine the buyer.<br/>


## Scarcity Auction

A gaming company auctions 100,000 game consoles with a set reserve price, while offering an additional 10,000 consoles for random sale to all participating consumers. The auction lasts for 2 hours.<br/>

After the auction ends, 10,000 game consoles are first allocated for random sale to consumers at the reserve price. Following this, the top 100,000 consumers with the highest bids will acquire the game consoles at a uniform purchase price equal to the bid of the 100,000th consumer.<br/>
