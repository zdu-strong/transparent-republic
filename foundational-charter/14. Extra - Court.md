# Court

The government establishes a unified body of law, a Supreme Court, and National Circuit Courts across the country. These courts are responsible for accepting lawsuits and hearing cases.<br/>


## National Circuit Court

Each National Circuit Court is equipped with at least two panels of judges. When parties to a case adjudicated by one panel initiate a second instance appeal, the appeal is tried by the other panel of judges.<br/>

Each panel adopts a Tri-Vector Commander format, consisting of one judge trying the case and two supervisors, with all three holding the power to vote and switch the presiding judge.<br/>

If a change of judge occurs in open court during the proceedings of a case, the new judge shall retry the case from the beginning.<br/>
